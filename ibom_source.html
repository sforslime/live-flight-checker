How Can We Help?

Check-in

Check-in

Airport Check-in begins 2 hours and closes 45 minutes before scheduled departure time. Online check-in begins 24 hours and closes 2 hours to schedule departure time for domestic flights; and 3 hours to schedule departure time for international flights.

Learn More

Baggage

Baggage

Passengers are allowed to check-in any number of bags weighing a total of 20kg in Economy Class & weighing a total of 30kg in Premium Economy Class. All passengers are allowed ONE carry-on bag.
